# Fake utilization estates: data dictionary

Generated by `scripts/demo/export_fake_estate.py` from the modules under
`finops_fakes/`. Both paths changed on 2026-09-21 with ADR-0022. Two years of hourly data ending at `fixtures.AS_OF`
(2024-08-24T06:00Z to 2026-08-24T06:00Z), five accounts, three fictional
regions. Deterministic: two runs at the same `--at` are byte identical, and
`manifest.json` carries a SHA-256 per file.

## Conventions

- **Null** is an empty unquoted field. No value is an empty string, so
  `COPY ... WITH (FORMAT csv, HEADER true)` reads nulls correctly.
- **Timestamps** are ISO 8601 UTC. An hourly point's `at` is the start of its
  hour; its `as_of` is `at + 1 hour` and is not repeated per row. `collected_at`
  is `fixtures.COLLECTED_AT` throughout.
- **Money** does not appear. Cost comes from `cur.py` and joins on `resource_id`.
- **JSON columns** (`tags`, `configuration`) are compact, key-sorted JSON.
- **`tenant_id`** is `fixtures.TENANT_ID` on every row that has one.

## The columns that answer "configured versus used"

`resource_inventory.configuration` holds the provisioned size: `instance_type`,
`vcpu`, `memory_gib`, `storage_gib`, `size_gib`, `volume_type`, `iops`,
`node_type`, `capacity_type`, `min_size`, `max_size`, `cloudwatch_agent`,
`burstable`, `attached_to`, `retention_days`, and so on, by resource type.

Use comes from the hourly series:

`container_usage_daily` compares per pod: `cpu_request_millicores` against
`cpu_used_avg_millicores` and `cpu_used_max_millicores`, and
`memory_request_mib` against `memory_used_peak_mib`. Requests are null on
`batch-eks`. `cpu_used_max_millicores` is the largest hourly maximum in the day.
The 99th percentile TASK-P6-007 sizes at is for the detector to compute over the
hourly series; a daily column cannot honestly hold it.

Absent is not zero anywhere: no memory series for `legacy-app` (no agent), no
volume series for an unattached volume, no request on `batch-eks`, no usage for
unmeasurable quotas, nothing at all for `fake-product-denied` or `fake-region-c`.

## Loading into Postgres

```bash
psql "$DB" -c "\copy topology_node FROM 'topology_node.csv' WITH (FORMAT csv, HEADER true)"
gunzip -c container_usage_daily.csv.gz | psql "$DB" -c "\copy container_usage FROM STDIN WITH (FORMAT csv, HEADER true)"
```

Column names follow the plan documents where the plans name them and are
proposals where they do not. Check each table's migration before loading.

The files are build output. Keep them out of git: `tenant_id` ends in a
twelve digit group, and byte counts such as `FreeStorageSpace` are twelve
digits, and `check_hardcoded.py` would stop the commit.

## Decisions this estate forces early

These are not resolved here. Each is a place where generating the data required
a choice the plans have not made yet.

1. **`MetricPoint` names no resource.** It is deliberately thin until the sub
   project that first reads a series widens it, TASK-P0-007. The generator
   carries the resource beside the point in `SeriesPoint` and changes nothing in
   `finops_core`. TASK-P3-007 has to add `resource_id` and dimensions.
2. **`ResourceRef` has no configuration.** Sizes travel in
   `utilization.configuration()`. `topology_node` is the likely home, TASK-P4-001.
3. **`UNREAD` needs a cause, and the six errors have none for "never tried".**
   The constraint in TASK-P4-002 requires a cause on every non complete slice.
   The fake uses `NotYetAttempted`, which is outside the closed taxonomy in
   TASK-P0-005 and would fail `test_taxonomy_is_closed` if added there. Either
   the coverage cause is its own enumeration or the constraint exempts `UNREAD`.
4. **Where does an observed OOM come from?** TASK-P6-004 says Container Insights
   never reliably reports it, so the adapter always sets unobserved. TASK-P6-005
   wants all three states. The fake gives true and false on the enhanced cluster
   and null on the basic one, so something other than basic Container Insights
   must be the source of true and false.
5. **Enumerations are proposals.** Node kinds, edge kinds and `usage_source`
   values are named here because the plans do not name them.
6. **The bill and the utilization estate join.** Closed 2026-09-21, and this
   entry is kept because the shape of the fix matters. It was the largest gap:
   `cur.py` billed one EC2 and one RDS resource per account for three months while
   the estate had 36 resources for two years, so only `amazonec2/<account>/0001`
   and `amazonrds/<account>/0001` appeared in both, and TASK-P3-013 worked around
   it by seeding three resources of its own with hand written charges.

   The bill now derives its services from `utilization.SERVICE_BY_PREFIX`, emits one
   line per estate resource, and prices each from that resource's own configuration
   through `finops_fakes/prices.py`. `estate_metrics.py` reads the same module, so
   the estimated saving and the charge it is about cannot disagree about what a
   thing costs. Two tests hold it: every estate resource is on the bill, and every
   billed resource is in the estate.

   `cur.LANDED` is the second half. Three resources change what they cost and what
   they consume from a fixed day onward, by different amounts, so the realized
   savings collector reaches VERIFIED, REGRESSED and INCONCLUSIVE from the charges
   rather than from a fixture written to produce them. A bill whose usage never
   moved could not produce the third verdict at all.

   `estate()` takes a period count, and the seed asks for five: a before window
   thirty days back from a change, and an after window ending sixty days forward,
   is ninety days and does not fit in three calendar months with any margin.

7. **No plan table stores raw metrics.** P3 reads `GetMetricData` and writes
   recommendations. `metric_point_hourly` is for the fake metric source, a time
   series store, or analysis, not for a Meter table.
8. **A disaster recovery replica is quiet by design.** Whether P3 reads a
   `purpose` tag to suppress it is for the P3 spec. The manifest expects no
   finding and says why.
9. **"Unqueried in 90 days" belongs to P2's evidence tables**, TASK-P3-009. Here
   it is `last_queried_days_ago` in the log group's configuration, to be mapped
   onto P2's shape.
10. **Quota codes prefixed `FAKE-` are placeholders.** The others are Service
    Quotas codes as best known and unverified, ISS-122.

## What is planted

`planted_findings.json` lists 58 entries, each with the plan task it serves, its
subjects, and the outcome a correct consumer reaches: `FINDING`, `NO_FINDING`,
`PRESSURE`, `REFUSE`, `UNOBSERVED` or `COVERAGE`. A detector test should assert
it found exactly the `FINDING` set, none of the `NO_FINDING` set, and refused or
qualified the rest.

One entry was not planted: `QUOTA_HELD_BY_IDLE_RESOURCE`. The untagged account
sits at 94 percent of its On-Demand vCPU limit, and 16 of those vCPU are the idle
`poc-ml-box`. It came out of deriving quota usage from the estate rather than
writing it down.
