## What it does

Attributes cost to a team, environment or customer through tags and Cost Categories, with an explicit and visible unallocated bucket.

## Why it exists

Cost that cannot be attributed cannot be acted on. The unallocated bucket is shown precisely so that it shrinks.

## What is already known

Target is above 95 percent of amortized cost allocated, tracked as a success criterion in the goal document.
